TO fish or not to fish

A side scrolling game where you play as a character on their fishing trip. Where the player will be able to catch fish via a minigame and be able to sell the fish for money to pay for upgrades, housing upgrades, and unlock specific upgrades to unlock new fish and chances. When the player unlocks enough money the game will have its final ending. This option shows up after you collect all the fish.
Certain upgrades and fish are only available at night where the player will get tired and require caffeine.
